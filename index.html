How a session works

  1. FIND

    Search by what you actually need

    Browse 28 categories, from prompt engineering and AI safety to tax, design, and negotiation. Read reviews left after real sessions and save the people you want to come back to.

  2. BOOK

    Take an open slot on their calendar

    Sessions run in 30- or 60-minute blocks against the expert's real availability, in your timezone. Your card is authorized when you book, not charged.

  3. TALK

    Call inside the app

    Voice calls run inside ClickMe by default, so neither of you hands over a phone number — or you can agree on Skype or Zoom instead. Either way, messages stay in one thread before and after.

  4. AFTER

    Pay for the time you used

    The charge goes through once the session ends. If it doesn't happen, you aren't charged for it.

What's in the app

Real availability

You see the slots an expert actually keeps open, with breathing room between sessions.

Private voice calls

In-app voice by default, with Skype or Zoom if you both prefer it. No phone numbers change hands.

Messages that stay put

One thread per expert, so context from your last session is still there.

Reviews from real sessions

Only people who booked and talked can leave one.

Saved cards

Pay with a saved card next time. Card details are handled by Stripe.

Reschedule when plans move

Propose a new time and the other person accepts or declines it.
