Privacy Policy

This policy explains what ClickMe collects when you use the app, why we collect it, and who else sees it.

Before you publish: this draft describes how the ClickMe app and backend actually handle data, but it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Have counsel review it, and fill in every placeholder, before you submit to the App Store.

What we collect

Account information

When you create an account we store your email address, your name, your username, and a profile photo if you upload one. Experts also provide the topics they take questions on, their rates, and the hours they make available.

Sessions and messages

We store your bookings — who you booked, when, and for how long — along with messages you send in the app and reviews you leave after a session. Voice calls are carried live and are not recorded or stored by us.

Payment information

Card details are collected and stored by Stripe, our payment processor. They do not pass through or rest on ClickMe's servers. We keep a record of each transaction — the amount, currency, status, and which booking it belongs to — so you and the expert can see your history.

Expert payout information

Experts who take payment complete identity verification with Stripe, which may include government identification and a tax identification number as required by law. Stripe holds this information; ClickMe stores only the resulting verification status and the identifiers needed to route payouts.

Device and notification data

If you turn on notifications we store a device token so we can send them. We also record ordinary technical information such as your device type and app version.

Why we use it

  • To run the service: making bookings, connecting calls, delivering messages, and handling payments
  • To notify you about bookings, messages, and payouts, in line with the notification settings you choose
  • To keep the service safe, investigate abuse, and resolve payment disputes
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers that make the app work:

  • Stripe — payments, expert identity verification, and payouts
  • Agora — carrying voice calls between you and the expert
  • Supabase — database and account hosting
  • Apple and Google — delivering push notifications to your device
  • Railway — application hosting

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

What the other person sees

Experts you book see your name, your profile photo, your booking details, and anything you write to them. Other users see reviews you leave, along with your name and photo. Your email address is never shown to another user.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Records of payments, payouts, and tax information are kept for as long as financial and tax law requires, which can be longer than your account lasts.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile information at any time in the app
  • Choose which notifications you receive, by category, in your settings
  • Request deletion of your account from within the app
  • Ask for a copy of your information by writing to us

Deleting your account removes your profile and stops the service. Records we are legally required to retain — chiefly payment and tax records — are kept for the required period.

Children

ClickMe is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
