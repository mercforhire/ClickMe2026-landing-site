3. How bookings work

In Short: a client requests a slot, the expert accepts or declines, and nothing is charged until after the session.

A client requests a slot with an expert, and the expert accepts or declines. If the expert does not accept within 12 hours, the request expires and the hold on your card is released. Accepting a request does not charge your card.

Each expert sets the length of every topic's session, in 30-minute steps, and its price. Paid sessions can be booked up to 6 days ahead. Some topics are free. Free topics involve no payment.

The meeting is an in-app voice call, or an external Skype or Zoom link that the expert supplies. Skype and Zoom are third-party services with their own terms.

Rescheduling

Either party can propose a new time. Only the other party can accept or decline the proposal. Nothing changes until they accept. A paid session can only move to a time within 6 days of now.

4. Prices and payments

In Short: you pay the listed price, your card is held when you book, and it is charged after the session.

Experts set their prices in CAD or USD. USD prices are shown as "US$". The minimum price for a paid session is $5. Promo codes, either a percentage or a fixed amount, may apply.

You pay exactly the listed price, with no added booking or service fee. Any taxes that apply will be shown before you pay.

Stripe processes payments. Card details are held by Stripe. The charge is made on the expert's Stripe account: the expert is the seller of the session, and ClickMe facilitates it.

When your card is held and charged

Your card is authorised, which places a hold on it, when you book. It is charged about 15 minutes after the session's scheduled end. Holds last about 7 days, so if a hold is about to lapse, we may charge an eligible session earlier.

If a confirmed booking is rescheduled beyond the hold window, we release the hold and charge your saved card about 24 hours before the session. You may be asked to complete card authentication within 6 hours. If that charge fails, we cancel the booking and notify you both.

If authorisation fails when you book, no booking is made.

5. Cancellations, no-shows and refunds

In Short: cancelling before the session means you are not charged, and an absent expert means you are not charged either.

  • If you are the client: you can cancel until the session's scheduled end. If your card is only held, the hold is released and nothing is charged. If it was already charged, you get a full refund if you cancel more than 24 hours before the start, and otherwise there is no refund. In practice the charge happens after the session, so cancelling beforehand releases the hold.
  • If you are the expert: you can cancel at any time. The client is not charged, or is fully refunded.
  • If the expert does not show, or nobody joins, the session is marked missed and the client is not charged, or is fully refunded.

If the client does not show

If the expert joined and the client did not, the client is charged. After the session, the expert may mark it completed or missed and name the no-show party. The client is charged when the session is marked completed or as a client no-show. If you think that is wrong, you can contact support to dispute it.

To ask for a refund, email clickmeapp@uptrendinvestments.net. Refunds go back to the original card.
