Delete your ClickMe account

ClickMe is published on Google Play by Uptrend Investments Inc. This page explains how to delete your ClickMe account and your data, with or without the app, what is deleted, and what we are required or need to keep.

Delete in the app

  1. Open ClickMe and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Security & Password.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Read the warning, then tap Delete Account. (Tap Keep my account to cancel.)
  6. Choose the main reason you are leaving. Additional comments are optional.
  7. Under Confirm your password, enter your password.
  8. Tap Delete Account. The farewell screen confirms that your account has been deleted.

Ask us to delete it (no app needed)

If you cannot or do not want to use the app, email us from the email address that is on your ClickMe account:

clickmeapp@uptrendinvestments.net with the subject Delete my ClickMe account.

  • The request must come from the email address on the account. We do not delete an account on a request from any other address.
  • We reply to that address to confirm the request before we delete anything.
  • We delete the account within 30 days of your confirmation.

Before you delete

Cancel or finish your upcoming sessions first. An account with an upcoming booked session, a session in progress, or a session starting within the next 15 minutes cannot be deleted yet. If you are an expert, this includes booking requests that are waiting for your answer.

Booking requests that you made as a client and that are still waiting for the expert to accept are cancelled when the account is deleted, and the card authorization for them is released.

What we delete

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. After deletion the same email address can be used to sign up for a new account.

  • Your profile. Your name is replaced by "Deleted user", and your email address is replaced so that the original address is released. Your photo, bio, job title, company, city, state or province, country and time zone are cleared.
  • Expert profile details. Your education and your meeting link are cleared.
  • Notes on bookings. The notes you wrote as a client on a booking are cleared. As an expert, your message, private note, key takeaways, internal and proposed notes, decline text and meeting link on bookings are cleared.
  • Chat previews. Where your message was the latest in a chat, the preview shown in the chat list is replaced by "[Message from deleted account]".
  • Notification data. Your push notification device registrations, your notifications and your notification preferences are deleted.
  • Discovery activity. Your activity in the expert discovery feed, and the discovery activity other people had with your expert profile, are deleted.
  • Security records. Any password-reset and account-deletion tokens are deleted.
  • Uploaded files. Your profile photo and the images you sent in chats are removed from our storage.
  • Sign-in. Sign-in to the account is blocked and your signed-in sessions are ended.

What we keep

  • Reviews, chat messages and reports. Reviews you wrote or received, messages you sent in chats and reports involving your account stay in place. They are shown as coming from "Deleted user", and your name, photo and email are no longer attached to them.
  • Booking and payment records. Records of bookings, payments, payouts and taxes are kept for as long as financial and tax law requires, and are no longer linked to a name or email address on your profile.
